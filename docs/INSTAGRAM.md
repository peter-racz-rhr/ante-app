## 2. The first post: a 7-slide carousel

Carousels get the most reach for new accounts: people swipe, and every swipe counts as time spent
on the post. The first slide is the hook and has to work without the rest.

### Posting checklist

- Post Tuesday to Thursday, 18:00 to 20:00 Budapest time, when people are scrolling after work.
- For the first hour, reply to every comment within minutes; early replies push the post further.
- Share the post to your Story with a poll sticker: *"Would you bet your screen time?" Yes / Never*.
- Add a song in the carousel editor (any trending, calm track). Carousels with music can show up
  in the Reels feed.
- Turn the "BETA" comments into DMs with the closed-test link. Those are your 12 Google testers.

## 4. Engagement playbook

- **Hook first:** the first line of every slide or Reel has to work without context. Use a claim,
  a POV, or a number.
- **One idea per post.** If you need "and", make two posts.
- **Talk like a person**, not a brand: "I built", "my screen time", "be honest".
- **Comment-to-get** ("comment BETA") beats "link in bio" for reach, because every comment counts.
- **Reply with questions.** A reply that asks something back doubles the thread.
- **Save-worthy beats like-worthy:** tips, checklists and the "5 tricks" carousel get saved, and
  saves are the strongest signal.
- **Post 3 times a week for 4 weeks** before judging anything. The first posts reach almost only
  your followers.
- **Spend 15 minutes a day** commenting thoughtfully on posts in #digitalwellbeing and #indiedev.
  New accounts grow by showing up in other people's comments.
- **Look at Insights after 7 days:** the reach from non-followers tells you which hook to repeat.
- **Never buy followers or use follow/unfollow apps.** Instagram detects them and stops showing
  your posts.
