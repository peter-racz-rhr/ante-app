# Terms of Service

These terms apply when you use the Ante app and its web demo. Last updated 1 November 2026. You accept them by tapping "I'm 18 or older and agree" when you first open Ante.

## 1. What Ante is

Ante is a screen-time tool. You set daily time limits for apps on your phone. When a limit is used up, Ante covers that app. The tables (roulette, blackjack, slots, poker and coin flip) let you stake minutes of that time budget.

## 2. No real money, no prizes

Ante is not a gambling service. You cannot pay money into the tables, and you cannot win money, goods or anything else of value. Minutes won or lost only change the time limits you set yourself, and they reset every day. Every table returns a little less than it takes on average. That is a design choice so that playing costs minutes rather than adding them; it is not a promise that your screen time goes down.

## 3. Who can use it

You need to be at least 18 years old, because the tables simulate casino games. If gambling-style games are a problem for you, or might become one, please don't use the tables. If gambling, even with time, starts to feel like a problem, talk to your doctor or a support service near you.

## 4. Your phone and its settings

Blocking needs Android's accessibility permission, which you can turn on and off in Settings at any time. Ante never covers your phone app, your messages app or emergency calls, whatever your limits say. Ante can't guarantee that every app is blocked in every situation, for example web versions of apps or picture-in-picture video. Strict mode puts a 30-second wait in front of Settings, the Play Store and uninstalling. A timed Strict lock that you start yourself can't be switched off early in the app; it ends by itself at the time you chose. In the version from Google Play you can always uninstall Ante after the wait; versions installed outside Google Play keep the uninstall screen covered until the lock ends. It is friction you agreed to, not a guarantee: Android can always be started in safe mode.

## 5. Your account and your data

Signing in with Google is optional. It puts your name and email on this phone, and lets you back up your data to your own Google Drive and restore it on another phone. You can sign out, download your data or delete all of it at any time in Settings → Your data. Deleting waits until a Strict lock or Focus that you started has ended.

## 6. Ante Supporter and tips

Ante Supporter is an optional one-time purchase through Google Play that unlocks extra looks (themes, clock fonts, table felts, card backs and a badge). It never unlocks blocking features, minutes or better odds. Tips through Google Play or Ko-fi are voluntary gifts and unlock nothing. Google Play handles payments, refunds and taxes under its own terms. A Supporter purchase is linked to your Google account and can be restored on a new phone. Right of withdrawal: Ante Supporter is digital content delivered right away. By buying it you ask for it to be delivered immediately and acknowledge that you then lose the 14-day right of withdrawal. Your rights if it doesn't work as described (warranty for digital content) are not affected. After buying, Ante shows a confirmation with the product, price, date and your request for immediate delivery, which you can save or share; it stays under Settings → Support Ante → Receipt.
