# Delete your Ante data

Ante has no server and no Ante account. Everything it stores is on your phone, plus an optional
backup file in your own Google Drive.

**In the app:** Settings → Your data → **Delete my data**. This deletes your limits, screen-time
history, table results, schedules, name, photo, Google sign-in and settings on the phone. Leave
"Also delete the Google Drive backup" on to delete the backup file too. If a Strict lock or Focus
you started is running, deleting waits until it ends.

**Without the app:** uninstall Ante, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Ante → Storage → Clear
storage. To delete the Drive backup, open drive.google.com → Settings (gear) → Manage apps → Ante →
Options → **Delete hidden app data**. To remove Ante's access to your Google account, go to
myaccount.google.com → Security → Third-party connections → Ante → Delete all connections.

**Purchases:** Ante Supporter is kept by Google Play with your Google account, not by Ante.
