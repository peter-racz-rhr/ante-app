## Colours

The Latte, Frappé, Macchiato and Mocha themes use the Catppuccin palettes (MIT licence).

## Icons

Line icons drawn for Ante; the settings cog follows Lucide's "settings" icon (ISC licence).

## Design tools

Animated product scenes follow the Stageshow guidelines by Benji Taylor (MIT licence). Also used: ECC (Everything Claude Code) and Matt Pocock's skills for planning and reviews.

## Built with

Android, Kotlin, Credential Manager and a WebView; tested with Playwright; written together with Claude.
