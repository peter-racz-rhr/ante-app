# Credits

Ante is built by one person with the help of free, open work by others. Thank you.

## Fonts

Geist (interface) by Vercel. Clock fonts: Bitcount by Petr van Blokland, Doto by Óliver Lalan, DotGothic16 by Fontworks, Handjet by Rosetta, Workbench and Sixtyfour by Jens Kutilek, Silkscreen by Jason Kottke, Pixelify Sans by Stefie Justprince, VT323 by Peter Hull. All under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

## Handwriting

Péter's letter to Supporters is set in Caveat by Pablo Impallari (SIL Open Font License 1.1).

## Colours

The Latte, Frappé, Macchiato and Mocha themes use the Catppuccin palettes (MIT licence).

## Website lists

The adult and gambling site lists come from The Block List Project (github.com/blocklistproject/Lists) and Steven Black's hosts (github.com/StevenBlack/hosts), both under the MIT licence.

## Icons

Line icons drawn for Ante; the settings cog follows Lucide's "settings" icon (ISC licence).

## Design tools

Animated product scenes follow the Stageshow guidelines by Benji Taylor (MIT licence). Also used: ECC (Everything Claude Code) and Matt Pocock's skills for planning and reviews.

## Built with

Android, Kotlin, Credential Manager and a WebView; tested with Playwright; written together with Claude.
