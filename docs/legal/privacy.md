# Privacy Policy

How Ante handles your data. Last updated 2 November 2026. In short: everything stays on your phone, including the website filter. The only server is a small one that checks Ko-fi donations, and it never sees your email address.

## What stays on your phone

Your limits, the time left and used per app, your daily history (about six months), your table results, your settings, your theme and, if you add one, your profile picture (a small copy made from the photo you pick). This is stored on your device. It only leaves it if you back it up to your own Google Drive (see below). Legal basis: providing the app you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

## App updates

Ante checks the project's public GitHub page (github.com) for a newer version of its screens, at most every ten minutes while you use it, and downloads it if there is one. These requests carry no personal data, but GitHub, like any website, sees your IP address and keeps standard logs under its own privacy policy (docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies). Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the app up to date and secure (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Each update is signed, and Ante refuses anything that isn't.

## Which app is in front

With the accessibility permission on, Android tells Ante the name of the app that comes to the front, so Ante can cover apps that are over their limit. Ante does not read what is on your screen, what you type or your notifications. The app names are used on the phone and not stored beyond your usage totals. Which apps you use, and for how long, can say a lot about you (a dating, health or religious app, for example), which is why this history stays on your phone and, if you choose, in your own Drive.

## Google sign-in (optional)

If you sign in with Google, Google shows its own sign-in screen and gives Ante your name, email address and account picture. Ante downloads the picture once from Google's image server and saves a small copy, with your name and email, on your phone. Google processes this sign-in under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Ante does not keep the Google sign-in token. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you can withdraw by signing out.

## The app list

To let you pick apps, Ante reads the list of apps installed on your phone and their icons. This list stays on the phone.

## Website filter (optional)

If you turn on the website filter, Android asks you once to allow Ante's VPN. It carries only DNS lookups: the names of the sites that apps and browsers on your phone want to reach. Ante checks each name on the phone against lists that come with the app (adult sites, gambling sites and the sites you add). Blocked names get a "no such site" answer; all other lookups go on to your network's usual DNS server, as they would without Ante. Ante does not store, log or send which sites you visit, and no other traffic goes through it. Legal basis: providing the feature you turned on (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

## Web demo

The web demo runs in your browser with sample data and stores its state in your browser's local storage. Wherever it is hosted, the website host may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy.

## Google Drive backup (optional)

If you turn on backup or tap Back up, Ante asks Google for access to a private app folder in your own Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission: Ante can't see any of your other files). It saves one file there with your limits, schedules, history, table results and settings. The file goes straight from your phone to Google and never passes through a server of Ante's. Legal basis: your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Delete my data removes it, and you can remove Ante's access in your Google account under Security → Third-party connections.

## Ko-fi donations and the Supporter check (optional)

If you donate on Ko-fi, Ko-fi sends Ante's small supporter server (run on Cloudflare Workers) a notice of the donation. The server keeps only a one-way fingerprint (SHA-256) of the email address you used, the date of your first and latest donation, how many you made and their total. It does not keep your name, your email address, your message or any payment details. When you tap "I donated, check" (and about once a week after that), Ante sends the same fingerprint of your Google sign-in email to the server, which only answers yes or no. Legal basis: legitimate interest in thanking supporters (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). To have your entry removed, write to the email address above.

## Ko-fi

The Ko-fi link opens ko-fi.com in your browser. Anything you do there is handled by Ko-fi under its own privacy policy (ko-fi.com/home/privacy).

## Transfers outside the EU

Google (sign-in, Drive backup), GitHub (updates) and Cloudflare (the supporter check) are US companies. Data that reaches them is transferred under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, which all three are certified under, and their standard contractual clauses.

## How long

On-device data stays until you delete it in Settings → Your data, uninstall Ante or clear its data in Android settings. A Drive backup stays until you delete it. Daily history is limited to about six months. Signing out deletes your name and email right away.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, to data portability and to withdraw consent. You can do most of this yourself in Settings → Your data: Download my data (access and portability) and Delete my data (erasure). For anything else write to peter.racz.rhr@gmail.com.
